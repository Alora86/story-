Our Story 📖

From one little message to a thousand memories...

OCTOBER 30, 2021 💌

The First Message

Do you remember the day we first started talking? It was during COVID, when I was in ninth grade. One little message started everything. We decided to become best friends, without knowing how important we would eventually become to each other. Looking back now, I never imagined that one simple conversation would become such a beautiful part of my life.

OUR FIRST CALL ☎️

That Shy Little Voice

I still remember when you asked if you could call me. You were so shy that you barely knew how to talk to a girl on the phone, and that made the moment even more memorable. When school reopened, you were right there in my class, but I didn't even recognise you! I waited to hear your name during attendance, only to discover that there were two Abhis in our class. Even now, these little memories make me smile.

FEBRUARY 23, 2022 ❤️

The Beginning Of Us

Even when we were just friends, I felt sad whenever you didn't come to class. I used to get jealous when you talked to other girls, and sometimes we fought over the smallest things. When you first told me about your feelings, I said no because I was afraid of losing our friendship. But you kept showing me how much I meant to you. Eventually, I said yes, and February 23, 2022, became one of the most special dates in my life. That was the beginning of our story as a couple. 💗

A MEMORY I WILL KEEP FOREVER 🫶

The First Time You Held My Hand

Do you remember when we went to look at schools for our plus one studies? That day, you held my hand. You probably didn't realise how much that little moment meant to me. Holding your hand was something I had secretly wished for. I was so happy that day, and later I told you just how much that moment meant to me. It might have been a small thing to you, but it became a beautiful memory for me. ❤️

OUR LITTLE FIRSTS 💋

Moments That Became Memories

I will always remember our first kiss during that special class. It was unexpected, and it was a first for both of us. I still remember secretly putting a sweet in your mouth during our class ten farewell, when nobody noticed. And on my birthday, you gave me a gift. When we went near your gym, you gave me my first kiss on the lips. These moments may seem small to the world, but they hold a special place in my heart. They are little pieces of our story that I never want to forget. 🥹💗

EVEN WHEN THINGS GOT HARD 🌧️

We Had Our Difficult Days Too

Our story hasn't always been easy. We have argued, misunderstood each other, and become upset over little things. Sometimes I got scared when my family found out about us. Sometimes I said we should break up because I didn't know what else to do. We also felt sad when we couldn't study at the same school or college, even though we both wanted to be together. There were moments when we stopped talking, but somehow we found our way back to each other. Our relationship isn't perfect, but every experience has taught me something about us. ❤️

WHAT I REALISED 💗

You Mean So Much To Me

When we stopped talking for a while, I realised just how much I missed you. I understood how deeply I cared about you and how much your presence meant to me. Some of the happiest moments of my life have been the moments I spent with you. Even when things were difficult, being able to talk to you and share my day made me happy. You have become such an important part of my life, and I am grateful for all the memories we have made. You are one of the most precious gifts life has given me. 🥹❤️

A LITTLE PROMISE 💌

What I Want For Us

I don't expect us to have a perfect relationship. I just want us to understand each other, communicate when things get difficult, and never stop trying to make things better. I want us to laugh together, make silly memories, support each other, and learn from our mistakes. I know we may not always agree, but I hope we always remember why we chose each other. Thank you for being part of my life, Abhi. I hope our story has many more beautiful chapters waiting for us. ❤️
